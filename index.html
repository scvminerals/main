Mission & Vision

Africa holds nearly a third of the world’s mineral reserves and is among the fastest-growing regions on earth — yet only a fraction of the capital to develop them. We exist to help close that gap.

Our mission

Help the people who own Africa’s mineral assets turn them into lasting value — with the capability, capital and partnership that mid-size miners can’t reach alone.

Our vision

To become Sub-Saharan Africa’s leading mine-development partner: the first call for any license holder who wants to build, scale and bring a deposit to market.

We’re invested in more than minerals. Africa is one of the fastest-growing regions in the world, and its future matters to us — we want to help build it through deep, hands-on expertise in its resources.

Where we work
